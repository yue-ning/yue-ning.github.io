Our research is motivated by real-world problems in health informatics, the social sciences, and the natural sciences. We design faithful, causal, and robust machine learning methods, from LLM reasoning and agentic AI to graph and time-series learning, that discover dynamic and interpretable patterns in large-scale data.

I am proud of each one of my students and mentees. I enjoy working with all of them!

Where our alumni go: PhD graduates: Eindhoven University of Technology, AWS (Applied Scientist), Amazon Audible, Walmart Global Tech, Pathos AI. Undergraduate and high-school researchers: Capital One, Bank of America, Microsoft, and graduate or undergraduate study at CMU, MIT, Princeton, Duke, and UC Berkeley. Two students received the Stevens Excellence in Graduate Research Award.
